## Research scope

Reconstructed on **8 October 2026** from accessible public sources. These are selected historical intelligence reports, not a comprehensive social-listening study, competitor account audit or measured brand-performance analysis. Publication dates are checked; current pages may have been updated since the month concerned. Earlier contextual sources are labelled in each report. Publication after a report's month is excluded.

UAE, GCC and global evidence differs in coverage. Country-specific and aggregate examples are labelled; GCC examples do not claim coverage of all six countries. Company announcements and qualitative interviews are identified rather than treated as independent market measurements.

Recommendations are agency hypotheses for testing. No official brand guidelines, product claims, inventory, partnerships or campaign participation are assumed. Validate approved SKUs, claims, rights, availability and technical scripts before production. Historical prices and offers must not be reused as current promotions.
