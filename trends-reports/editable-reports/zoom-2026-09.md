Hook: What matters in your coffee moment?

Show an actual drink and approved preparation detail at a real store. Describe taste accurately without unsupported quality superiority.

Format: Reel | CTA: Tell us your coffee preference.

Production / checks: Confirmed SKU/store offer; all ingredients and allergens reviewed. Adult-facing execution unless child marketing is specifically approved.

### On the way, made useful

Hook: The stop that fits this journey.

Production / checks: Confirmed SKU/store offer; all ingredients and allergens reviewed. Adult-facing execution unless child marketing is specifically approved.
