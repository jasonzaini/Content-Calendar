## Proposed content briefs

### The everyday-use walkaround

Hook: Will this fit your actual day?

Use a real approved vehicle to demonstrate entry, boot space and one comfort feature while parked. Avoid implying it fits every family or comparing unmeasured dimensions.

Format: Reel | CTA: Ask for the vehicle details.

Production / checks: Qualified technical review; actual vehicle/service scope and disclosures. Customer or technician permissions; no unsafe demonstration.

### What to ask about a used EV

Hook: Before the badge, ask these questions.

Create a general buyer-education card reviewed by a qualified specialist. Do not suggest NXT has EV stock or promise a battery condition.

Format: Carousel | CTA: Save the checklist.

Production / checks: Qualified technical review; actual vehicle/service scope and disclosures. Customer or technician permissions; no unsafe demonstration.

### A real detail, a better question

Hook: Look here before the test drive.

Demonstrate one documented condition point on a real vehicle and explain what a buyer can verify. Confirm inventory status at publication.

Format: Short | CTA: Book an enquiry through the approved channel.

Production / checks: Qualified technical review; actual vehicle/service scope and disclosures. Customer or technician permissions; no unsafe demonstration.
