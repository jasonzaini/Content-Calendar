## Executive brief

Confidence through condition and cost transparency

- Show documented vehicle condition and clear buyer questions.

- Help buyers consider running costs without inventing savings.

- Use short walkarounds with real inventory and explicit disclosures.

## Proposed content briefs

### Five questions before a viewing

Hook: Before you ask the price, ask this.

Use one real vehicle to demonstrate buyer questions about condition, records and disclosed issues. Show evidence rather than staged assurance.

Format: Carousel | CTA: Save for your next viewing.

Production / checks: Qualified technical review; actual vehicle/service scope and disclosures. Customer or technician permissions; no unsafe demonstration.

### The detail-led walkaround

Hook: Look at the details that matter.

Open on a meaningful inspected detail, reveal the approved vehicle and show three documented features. Date inventory and price if shown.

Format: Reel | CTA: Enquire about this vehicle.

Production / checks: Qualified technical review; actual vehicle/service scope and disclosures. Customer or technician permissions; no unsafe demonstration.

### Price is one part of ownership

Hook: What belongs in your car budget?

Build a neutral ownership-cost diagram. Use customer-specific quotations only when verified and avoid guaranteed resale values.

Format: Carousel | CTA: Which cost would you like explained?

Production / checks: Qualified technical review; actual vehicle/service scope and disclosures. Customer or technician permissions; no unsafe demonstration.
