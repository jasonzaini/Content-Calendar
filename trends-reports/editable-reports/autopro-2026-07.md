## Proposed content briefs

### Ask the technician

Hook: What would you check before this journey?

A technician presents a safe, stationary scenario and explains one approved check. Avoid fake diagnoses or staged danger.

Format: Reel Q&A | CTA: Leave a question for the next episode.

Production / checks: Qualified technical review; actual vehicle/service scope and disclosures. Customer or technician permissions; no unsafe demonstration.

### Myth or model-specific?

Hook: Is this advice true for every car?

Explain a common maintenance statement and identify where manufacturer guidance differs. Keep the answer specific and expert-reviewed.

Format: Carousel | CTA: Save the reminder.

Production / checks: Qualified technical review; actual vehicle/service scope and disclosures. Customer or technician permissions; no unsafe demonstration.

### What is included?

Hook: What happens during this service?

Film the actual approved service sequence and describe its scope, exclusions and next steps. No guaranteed fuel-savings claim.

Format: Reel | CTA: Ask about the service.

Production / checks: Qualified technical review; actual vehicle/service scope and disclosures. Customer or technician permissions; no unsafe demonstration.
