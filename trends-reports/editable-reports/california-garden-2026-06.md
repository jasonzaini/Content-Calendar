## Proposed content briefs

### From can to gathering bowl

Hook: Start with this. Serve this.

Open on the finished dish, reveal a confirmed canned ingredient, show three real preparation steps and end with recipe quantities. Food handling requires a recipe review.

Format: Reel / Short | CTA: Save the recipe.

Production / checks: Confirmed SKU/store offer; all ingredients and allergens reviewed. Adult-facing execution unless child marketing is specifically approved.

### What is already in your pantry?

Hook: One pantry ingredient. Two meal directions.

Build two culturally suitable recipes from the same confirmed can. Photograph actual ingredients; distinguish added items from what comes in the pack.

Format: Carousel | CTA: Which version should we cook next?

Production / checks: Confirmed SKU/store offer; all ingredients and allergens reviewed. Adult-facing execution unless child marketing is specifically approved.

### The useful portion guide

Hook: How many servings does this recipe make?

Weigh recipe ingredients and plate the actual servings. Explain portions without nutrition promises or unsupported budget claims.

Format: Carousel | CTA: Share with your cooking partner.

Production / checks: Confirmed SKU/store offer; all ingredients and allergens reviewed. Adult-facing execution unless child marketing is specifically approved.
