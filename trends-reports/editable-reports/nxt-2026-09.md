## Proposed content briefs

### Prepared, not airbrushed

Hook: Here is how this vehicle is presented.

Show a real vehicle being cleaned under consistent light, then separately display verified condition information. Never equate cosmetic preparation with mechanical certification.

Format: Reel | CTA: Request the inspection details.

Production / checks: Qualified technical review; actual vehicle/service scope and disclosures. Customer or technician permissions; no unsafe demonstration.

### What retained value really means

Hook: An average is not your car’s future price.

Explain model-year and condition differences using a neutral diagram. Attribute the external dataset if mentioned; promise no resale figure.

Format: Carousel | CTA: Save before comparing cars.

Production / checks: Qualified technical review; actual vehicle/service scope and disclosures. Customer or technician permissions; no unsafe demonstration.

### The buyer question desk

Hook: What would help you decide?

Answer one actual buyer question with a relevant vehicle or general checklist. Verify powertrain and finance claims before publication.

Format: Reel Q&A | CTA: Enquire through the approved channel.

Production / checks: Qualified technical review; actual vehicle/service scope and disclosures. Customer or technician permissions; no unsafe demonstration.
