## Proposed content briefs

### The useful stop on the way

Hook: Before the errands, one useful stop.

Follow an adult through a realistic journey to a real ZOOM store. Show a confirmed coffee or food item and clear store context.

Format: Reel | CTA: Find the featured location.

Production / checks: Confirmed SKU/store offer; all ingredients and allergens reviewed. Adult-facing execution unless child marketing is specifically approved.

### Your routine, your coffee moment

Hook: Which moment is yours?

Film two adult routines and one actual drink. Use a relevant choice rather than targeting children or promising cognitive benefits.

Format: Story poll + Short | CTA: Choose your moment.

Production / checks: Confirmed SKU/store offer; all ingredients and allergens reviewed. Adult-facing execution unless child marketing is specifically approved.

### Before the road trip

Hook: What do you check before setting off?

Present a practical pickup list and a reminder to verify travel documents. No guaranteed entry, invented bundle or ongoing August offer.

Format: Carousel | CTA: Save the checklist.

Production / checks: Confirmed SKU/store offer; all ingredients and allergens reviewed. Adult-facing execution unless child marketing is specifically approved.
