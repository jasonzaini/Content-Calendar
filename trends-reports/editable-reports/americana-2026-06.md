## Proposed content briefs

### The small celebration edit

Hook: An ordinary evening. A little celebration.

Show an everyday table, reveal the actual cake pack, plate a realistic portion, then widen to an adult gathering. Keep pack, portion and serving honest.

Format: Reel / Short | CTA: What little moment would you celebrate?

Production / checks: Confirmed SKU/store offer; all ingredients and allergens reviewed. Adult-facing execution unless child marketing is specifically approved.

### One cake, two serving looks

Hook: Same cake. Which serving style is yours?

Film two plating treatments with the same confirmed product. Display added ingredients and allergens; invite an A/B choice without labelling either option healthier.

Format: Carousel + Story poll | CTA: Choose A or B.

Production / checks: Confirmed SKU/store offer; all ingredients and allergens reviewed. Adult-facing execution unless child marketing is specifically approved.

### The gathering starts at the table

Hook: Ready for the next get-together?

Capture hands preparing and sharing cake while a generic evening gathering unfolds. No match footage, crests or licensed music without clearance.

Format: Reel | CTA: Save this serving idea.

Production / checks: Confirmed SKU/store offer; all ingredients and allergens reviewed. Adult-facing execution unless child marketing is specifically approved.
