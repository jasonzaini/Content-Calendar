## Proposed content briefs

### The organised lunch-prep edit

Hook: A little preparation. A clearer week.

Create an adult meal-prep sequence using a verified canned ingredient, showing storage guidance reviewed for food safety. No implied school approval.

Format: Reel / Short | CTA: Save the recipe steps.

Production / checks: Confirmed SKU/store offer; all ingredients and allergens reviewed. Adult-facing execution unless child marketing is specifically approved.

### One ingredient, three prepared meals

Hook: Which meal belongs in your plan?

Build three genuinely different recipes with quantities and final portions. Keep the can’s contribution separate from added ingredients.

Format: Carousel + Story | CTA: Choose the next full recipe.

Production / checks: Confirmed SKU/store offer; all ingredients and allergens reviewed. Adult-facing execution unless child marketing is specifically approved.

### The recipe question desk

Hook: What would you like to cook with this?

Answer real preparation questions with a cook and a clearly identified product. Record assumptions and verify any nutrient language.

Format: Reel Q&A | CTA: Leave your cooking question.

Production / checks: Confirmed SKU/store offer; all ingredients and allergens reviewed. Adult-facing execution unless child marketing is specifically approved.
