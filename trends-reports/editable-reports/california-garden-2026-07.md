## Proposed content briefs

### One can, your next recipe

Hook: Which dish should we make next?

Present two recipe outcomes and the same verified canned ingredient. Use a poll where available and develop the winning option only after real responses.

Format: Short + Story | CTA: Choose the next recipe.

Production / checks: Confirmed SKU/store offer; all ingredients and allergens reviewed. Adult-facing execution unless child marketing is specifically approved.

### The practical ingredient edit

Hook: Here is what goes into the dish.

Show the pack, all added ingredients, quantities and cooking steps. Keep nutrition claims limited to verified product information.

Format: Carousel | CTA: Save the ingredient list.

Production / checks: Confirmed SKU/store offer; all ingredients and allergens reviewed. Adult-facing execution unless child marketing is specifically approved.

### Cook the answer

Hook: You asked how to use this.

Answer a genuine recipe question in a clearly demonstrated sequence. If no audience question exists, label it as a common preparation question rather than a real comment.

Format: Reel | CTA: Ask your cooking question.

Production / checks: Confirmed SKU/store offer; all ingredients and allergens reviewed. Adult-facing execution unless child marketing is specifically approved.
