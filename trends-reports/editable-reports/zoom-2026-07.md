## Proposed content briefs

### The coffee moment poll

Hook: Which moment needs your coffee stop?

Film two adult scenarios with one verified drink and one real store. Avoid suggesting everyone follows the same work hours.

Format: Short + Story poll | CTA: Choose your moment.

Production / checks: Confirmed SKU/store offer; all ingredients and allergens reviewed. Adult-facing execution unless child marketing is specifically approved.

### A stop, explained

Hook: Here is what this stop makes easy.

Show the entrance, actual product pickup and checkout in a short sequence. Confirm store opening information before publishing.

Format: Reel | CTA: Find the featured store.

Production / checks: Confirmed SKU/store offer; all ingredients and allergens reviewed. Adult-facing execution unless child marketing is specifically approved.

### The gathering pickup

Hook: On your way to the plans?

Show a realistic approved basket for a generic adult gathering. Do not invent bundles, discounts or tournament affiliation.

Format: Carousel | CTA: What would you add?

Production / checks: Confirmed SKU/store offer; all ingredients and allergens reviewed. Adult-facing execution unless child marketing is specifically approved.
