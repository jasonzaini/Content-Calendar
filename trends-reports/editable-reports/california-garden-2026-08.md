## Proposed content briefs

### The week starts in the pantry

Hook: One ingredient. Three meal directions.

Build three realistic dishes using a confirmed canned SKU, showing all added ingredients and measured portions. Separate each recipe’s requirements.

Format: Carousel | CTA: Save for your meal plan.

Production / checks: Confirmed SKU/store offer; all ingredients and allergens reviewed. Adult-facing execution unless child marketing is specifically approved.

### Show the flavour, not just the speed

Hook: Convenient can still look like this.

Open on the plated meal, reveal the can and show a simple preparation sequence. Make no unsupported cooking-time or nutrition claim.

Format: Reel / Short | CTA: Which recipe should we make next?

Production / checks: Confirmed SKU/store offer; all ingredients and allergens reviewed. Adult-facing execution unless child marketing is specifically approved.

### The transparent recipe basket

Hook: Here is what this meal actually uses.

List quantities and portions. If prices are included, source them on a stated date and explain exclusions; otherwise focus on use rather than a savings figure.

Format: Carousel | CTA: Share with the meal planner at home.

Production / checks: Confirmed SKU/store offer; all ingredients and allergens reviewed. Adult-facing execution unless child marketing is specifically approved.
