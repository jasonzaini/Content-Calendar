## Proposed content briefs

### A small pause, beautifully served

Hook: Make a little pause feel special.

Film an adult tea-break scene, actual cake portion and one simple plating move. Focus on texture and presentation without health language.

Format: Reel | CTA: Who would you share this moment with?

Production / checks: Confirmed SKU/store offer; all ingredients and allergens reviewed. Adult-facing execution unless child marketing is specifically approved.

### A real portion, a clear pack

Hook: Here is what you are serving.

Show the actual product, portion and added ingredients. Verify allergens and avoid presenting the cake as a balanced school meal.

Format: Carousel | CTA: Save the serving idea.
